# Money Learning Game

This context defines the shared language for a child-focused game that builds practical money-handling judgment through short learning experiences.

## Language

**Child Player**:
A child aged seven or older who plays independently and is comfortable with reading and mathematics at approximately a second-grade level.
_Avoid_: Student, user

**Parent**:
The single adult responsible for oversight of the single Child Player included in the first release.
_Avoid_: Administrator, supervisor

**Parent Account**:
The Parent's identity for accessing the game across supported devices; its sign-in is required on a new device before the Child Account can be used.
_Avoid_: Administrator account, household account

**Parent Area**:
A child-inaccessible part of the game where a Parent can review completed Lessons and skills needing practice, reset a Child Player's progress, and choose the Learning Currency.
_Avoid_: Admin panel, settings

**Goal-Based Money Choice**:
A decision between competing uses of limited game money, judged by how each option affects the Child Player's chosen goal rather than as universally right or wrong.
_Avoid_: Correct purchase, good spending

**Practice Money**:
A simulated balance with no real-world value, shown using the denominations of the selected Learning Currency.
_Avoid_: Game Coin, cash, real money

**Learning Currency**:
The real-world currency whose values and denominations appear in Lessons, selected by the Parent from United States dollars (USD), euros (EUR), or British pounds (GBP).
_Avoid_: EU currency, Game Coins

**Currency Switch**:
A change of Learning Currency that keeps the same numeric values for all Practice Money balances, prices, and goals while replacing their currency denomination one-to-one.
_Avoid_: Exchange-rate conversion, foreign exchange

**Child Account**:
The single Parent-created player identity in the first release, opened with a child PIN after the Parent Account has been authenticated and limited to a parent-chosen display name and the Child Player's learning progress.
_Avoid_: Child Profile, student record

**Closed Play Experience**:
A child-facing environment without real-money purchases, advertising, chat, social sharing, or external links, and without collection of the Child Player's real name, location, or photo.
_Avoid_: Social game, marketplace

**Lesson**:
A self-contained play session of about five minutes that presents an everyday story, lets the Child Player earn Practice Money and choose whether to spend or save it, shows the consequence, and offers a friendly recap and retry.
_Avoid_: Class, lecture

**Lesson Mastery**:
Demonstrated understanding when a Child Player makes a Goal-Based Money Choice, correctly identifies its effect on the selected goal, and, when feedback was needed, succeeds in a similar retry scenario.
_Avoid_: Lesson completion, correct spending choice

**Learning Path**:
The ordered progression from earning through effort, distinguishing needs from wants, spending within available Practice Money, saving toward a goal, and combining these skills in a simple plan.
_Avoid_: Course, curriculum

**Learning Reward**:
A star, badge, or friendly visual unlock that celebrates Lesson completion without competition, loss of progress, or daily-streak pressure.
_Avoid_: Prize money, leaderboard rank
